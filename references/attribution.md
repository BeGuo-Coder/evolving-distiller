# Design Attribution

This Skill is an independently rewritten synthesis inspired by three public MIT-licensed projects:

- `alchaincyf/darwin-skill`: validation-gated optimization, pairwise judge comparisons, keep/revert evolution, and audit-oriented iteration.
- `alchaincyf/nuwa-skill`: multi-source/multi-lens distillation, mental-model extraction, generativity/distinctiveness checks, and fidelity evaluation.
- `zjjoe2025/cangjie-skill-` (a fork of `Yeadon8888/cangjie-skill`): evidence-first extraction emphasizing belief-formation stories, real decisions, failures, contradictions, boundaries, and compact research records.

The implementation here does not copy the original SKILL.md text. It combines the design ideas into a runtime-neutral two-loop architecture: evidence-grounded distillation plus validation-gated evolution.
